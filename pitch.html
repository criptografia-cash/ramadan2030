How It Works

1

Each Surah = A Unique NFT Design

114 beautifully crafted animated artworks featuring Islamic geometric patterns, Arabic calligraphy, crescent moon, mosque silhouettes, and twinkling stars. Each NFT auto-plays the full Imam recitation of that Surah.

2

Supply = Number of Verses

The Quran itself determines rarity. Al-Kawthar has only 3 verses, so only 3 NFTs exist (Legendary). Al-Baqarah has 286 verses, so 286 NFTs exist (Common). The total across all Surahs is exactly 6,236.

3

Community Contributions, Not Fees

10% of every secondary sale is a community contribution: 5% to the creator, 5% to the Sadaqah Pool (community fund). No riba. No interest. Pure community support.

4

Musabaqah — The Blessed Raffle

At the first moon sighting of Ramadan in December 2030, one lucky NFT holder wins 50% of the entire Sadaqah Pool accumulated since launch. Transparent, on-chain, verifiable.
